01 // About

02 // Projects

Wheel Mount FEA Optimization

Simulation

Performed structural hand calculations to determine the precise resultant forces applied to a single rear wheel mount. Evaluated worst-case load conditions using the vehicle's maximum total mass, total wheel mount distribution, a design factor of safety of 3.0, and a 60/40 rear-to-front weight distribution ratio.

Imported the calculated force vectors into Ansys to execute a comprehensive Finite Element Analysis (FEA) simulating an accurate bearing load on the mount structure. Analyzed Von Mises stresses, Von Mises strains, and total structural deformation across the component to validate component integrity under maximum operating braking loads.

Robot Arm for Cinematography

Concept Design

Executed the structured conceptual development and parametric CAD optimization of a long-reach, six-axis robotic manipulator arm. Conducted extensive industrial benchmarking across 10+ commercial architectures to synthesize rigid quantitative engineering specifications, defining a reach greater than 1.5 meters, a payload capacity of 3 to 7 kilograms, and a total system mass under 100 kg while constraining the production budget under $3,000.

Performed structural torque, inertia, and load calculations to accurately size complex drivetrain subassemblies. The final engineering design utilized these calculations to specify a 75:1 planetary gearbox rated for 195 N-m and high-torque joints capable of delivering 450 N-m, generating detailed SolidWorks assembly models, detailed engineering drawings, and complete bills of materials using DFM, DFA, and GD&T principles.

Right-Angled Speed Reduction Gearbox

Rapid Prototyping

Collaborated within a four-member engineering team to design, model, and rapid-prototype a functional, 3D-printed right-angle speed reduction gearbox delivering a 3:1 mechanical reduction. Handled the parametric CAD generation of the assembly in SolidWorks, implementing custom design-for-manufacturing and design-for-assembly (DFM/DFA) principles optimized to eliminate external fasteners and achieve a total 3D printing time under 6 hours.

Engineered the physical layout for a completely tool-free assembly process while strictly maintaining the required speed reduction constraints. Physical testing and manufacturing validation successfully achieved an assembly time of under 1 minute by an independent evaluator using the generated engineering drawings and assembly manual.

SolidWorks 3D Printing Rapid Prototyping DFM/DFA

Apple Watch Stand

Product Design

Designed and rapid-prototyped a multi-device charging stand for consumer electronics, accommodating an Apple Watch and AirPods case within a compact, ergonomic desktop footprint. Handled the full parametric CAD modeling in SolidWorks, engineering custom geometric recesses for inductive charging pucks and integrated, press-fit cable routing channels to ensure clean wire management along the main support spine.

Leveraged additive manufacturing principles (3D printing) to iterate and fabricate the physical product, optimizing the model geometry to account for print tolerances, overhangs, and material constraints. Successfully validated the design through physical fitment testing, ensuring precise component tolerances, a stable center of gravity during device mounting, and seamless charging cable integration.

One-Cylinder Pneumatic Engine

Machining & Assembly

Fabricated and verified a high-tolerance, one-cylinder pneumatic engine to fulfill strict precision machining specifications. Translated formal engineering blueprints into physical hardware by operating manual mills, lathes, and precision drill presses to form eight distinct components out of aluminum and steel stocks.

Maintained critical interface tolerances within ±0.001 inches and achieved smooth surface finishes below 32 micro-inch Ra during the machining processes. Conducted formal functional testing with 40-100 psi compressed air to validate performance, successfully achieving 1,200 RPM during operation with complete mechanical reliability.
